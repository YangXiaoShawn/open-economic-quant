# Resume Bullets

- Evaluated linear, elastic-net, gradient-boosted tree, and neural-network factor timing across 801,536 country-factor-months (153 JKP factors, 22 emerging markets, 2000-2025 OOS), proving via Monte Carlo size/power simulations that standard historical-mean benchmarks suffer an 87.5% false-positive rate in multi-country panels.
- Showed that replacing the own-series mean with a regional shrinkage mean reduces nominal size to 5.0%, turns all 12 learned models out-of-sample $R^2_{\text{OS}}$ negative on value-weighted factors (-0.05% to -0.48%), and leaves a low-turnover shrinkage-mean tilt with the highest net Sharpe (1.91 vs 0.71-1.26 at 20 bp).
