## 4. Key Takeaways From the 2026 Out-of-Sample Extension (`2026-01` to `2026-10-21`)

1. **Cross-sectional shrinkage tilts (`shrink_mean`, `eb_shrink_mean`, `turnover_damped_blend`) continue to dominate out of sample in 2026**:
   - Every shrinkage rule beats both `own` historical mean (`R²_OS = +0.027%` to `+0.033%`, Clark–West $t = 1.62$ to $1.71$ over just 10 months) and the static equal-weight portfolio (`+6.33%` to `+6.95%` net vs `+4.07%` for `static_ew`), with 9 out of 10 positive months and virtually zero drawdown (`-0.01%` to `-0.12%`).
   - Across the full 322-month window (`2000-01` through `2026-10-21`), `shrink_mean:rule` ($\kappa=120$) increases its 20 bp net Sharpe from **1.9054** (`2025-12`) to **1.9594** (`2026-10-21`).
2. **Real-time forecast shrinkage (`_rs`) and turnover damping (`turnover_damped_blend`) protect ML models during the July & October 2026 factor reversals**:
   - In July 2026 (`2026-07`) and October 2026 (`2026-10-01 ~ 2026-10-21`, when Momentum reversed `-6.84%`), `fmom:rule` lost `-2.74%` and `-1.31%`, and unshrunk linear/DM-transfer models (`ols:dm_only`, `enet:dm_only`) lost `-2.69%` to `-2.72%` in July and `-0.85%` in October.
   - Forecast-shrunk models (`enet_rs:own` **`+5.78%`**, `ols_rs:own` **`+5.75%`**, `gbrt_rs:own` **`+4.86%`**) and **`turnover_damped_blend:own` (`+6.61%`)** cut turnover by half and outperformed `static_ew` (`+4.07%`) net of 20 bp costs.
